# Generative AI Playbook

## Use Case Identification
- Evaluate areas where generative AI can add value (e.g., content creation, customer service).
- Prioritize use cases based on impact and feasibility.

## Technology Selection
- Assess available generative AI models and platforms.
- Consider factors like performance, scalability, and integration capabilities.

## Implementation Strategy
- Develop a roadmap for pilot projects and scaling.
- Define success metrics and evaluation criteria.

## Ethical Considerations
- Establish guidelines to address biases and ensure responsible AI use.
- Monitor and audit AI outputs for compliance and quality.

## Training and Change Management
- Provide training for staff on generative AI tools and best practices.
- Manage organizational change to support AI adoption.
