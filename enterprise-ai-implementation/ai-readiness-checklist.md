# AI Readiness Checklist

## Organizational Readiness
- [ ] Executive sponsorship and strategic alignment
- [ ] Defined AI vision and objectives
- [ ] Cross-functional AI governance team established

## Data Readiness
- [ ] Inventory of available data sources
- [ ] Data quality and integrity assessments completed
- [ ] Data governance policies in place

## Technical Readiness
- [ ] Scalable infrastructure to support AI workloads
- [ ] Access to necessary AI tools and platforms
- [ ] Integration capabilities with existing systems

## Talent and Skills
- [ ] Skilled personnel in data science and AI
- [ ] Training programs for upskilling staff
- [ ] Recruitment strategies for AI talent

## Ethical and Legal Considerations
- [ ] Compliance with relevant regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA)
- [ ] Ethical guidelines for AI development and deployment
- [ ] Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
